## 🎮 About Ledify

**Ledify** is the best LED banner app that transforms your iPhone into a bright, scrolling LED display. Perfect for greeting loved ones at airports, showing excitement at concerts, celebrating birthdays, requesting songs from DJs, and expressing feelings to family and friends. With retro neon aesthetics and smooth scrolling animations, your messages will shine in any crowd!

### ⚡ Key Features

#### Free Features
- **📱 LED Scrolling Display** - Smooth scrolling text with bright LED-style visuals
- **🎨 Font Customization** - Multiple font formats and sizes to choose from
- **⚡ Speed Control** - Adjust scroll speed to match your needs
- **🌈 Color Options** - Customize background colors for maximum visibility
- **🌍 Multi-Language** - Support for Chinese, Korean, Japanese, German, French, Russian, Italian, Greek, Spanish, and more
- **😊 Emoji Support** - Access to thousands of emojis to enhance your messages

#### Pro Subscription Features
- **✨ Animated Backgrounds** - Dynamic backgrounds that make your message pop
- **💎 Text Shadows** - Add depth and readability to your display
- **📺 HD Resolution** - Crystal-clear display optimized for retina screens
- **🚫 No Ads** - Uninterrupted experience for important moments

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

Ledify combines the nostalgia of classic LED displays with modern technology:

- **Neon Color Palette** - Vibrant colors inspired by 80s/90s LED signs that pop in any lighting
- **Retro Aesthetics** - Scanlines, grid backgrounds, and CRT monitor effects
- **Smooth Scrolling** - Fluid text animations optimized for readability
- **High Visibility** - Bright displays designed to be seen from a distance
- **Intuitive Controls** - Quick customization to get your message up fast
- **Event-Ready** - Optimized for concerts, airports, and crowded venues
