🔒 Privacy Policy 🔒

Last Updated: February 2024

Introduction

Welcome to Ledify! We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information when you use our retro-styled finance tracking application.

Information We Collect

Ledify collects the following types of information:

  • Financial transaction data you manually enter into the app
  • Account categories and budget settings
  • App usage statistics and preferences
  • Device information for app optimization

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Provide you with accurate financial tracking and reporting
  • Generate personalized insights and spending patterns
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Sync your data across your devices (if enabled)

Data Storage & Security

We take your financial data security seriously:

  • All data is encrypted both in transit and at rest
  • We use industry-standard security protocols
  • Your financial data is stored locally on your device by default
  • Cloud sync is optional and uses end-to-end encryption

Third-Party Services

Ledify may integrate with third-party services:

  • Analytics services to improve app functionality
  • Cloud storage providers (only if you enable sync)
  • Banking APIs (with your explicit permission)

We do not sell or share your personal financial data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all data we store about you
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Export your financial data in standard formats
  • Opt out of analytics and data collection
  • Disable cloud sync at any time

Children's Privacy

Ledify is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes through the app or via email.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your data, please contact us at:
